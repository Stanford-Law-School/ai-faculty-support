## Use rules

- Begin with AI Essentials Training and Responsible AI at Stanford when institutional AI use is relevant.
- Use Lexis, Westlaw, Bloomberg Law, official government sources, and appropriate citators for authoritative legal research and updating.
- Treat Harvey, Legora, LexText, CICERO, and database AI features as assistants whose output still requires source-level verification.
- Use Stanford enterprise services only within their current documented terms and data boundaries.
- Never infer that a tool is approved for a particular dataset or workflow merely because it appears here.
- Use the access request form for library-provided legal AI tools and contact library@law.stanford.edu for help.
