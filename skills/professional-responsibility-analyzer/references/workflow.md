# Workflow

1. Confirm that the request is for research or teaching rather than advice in an active matter.
2. Identify each actor, role, client relationship, jurisdiction, tribunal, and relevant time.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, disputed facts, and facts that would change the analysis.
4. Identify possible duties without deciding the outcome prematurely.
5. Retrieve current rule text, comments, cases, ethics opinions, and other relevant authority.
6. Distinguish mandatory authority, advisory guidance, disciplinary precedent, and professional norms.
7. Map conflicts among duties and available procedural responses.
8. Identify supervision, disclosure, consent, withdrawal, reporting, or remedial questions.
9. State jurisdictional and factual uncertainty.
10. Produce an issue map or teaching hypothetical, not legal advice.
