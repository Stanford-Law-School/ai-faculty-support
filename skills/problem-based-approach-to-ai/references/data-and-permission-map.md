# Data and permission map

Identify what information and access a proposed use would require, and default to the minimum. This Skill identifies questions; it does not authorize data use or grant permissions.

## Map

- Required information (what the use actually needs)
- Source of that information
- Sensitivity (public, internal, confidential, restricted)
- Minimum necessary information (can less be used?)
- De-identification question (can identifiers be removed?)
- Account type (personal, enterprise, institutional)
- Storage location
- Retention
- Training or product-improvement use of inputs
- Human access by the provider
- Connectors in use
- External providers involved
- Read permissions
- Write permissions
- Logs
- Deletion process
- Export capability
- Institutional questions to resolve first

## Defaults

- Prefer public or synthetic material for exploration.
- Use the least data and the least access that can meet the need.
- De-identify wherever the task allows.

## Sensitive categories

Do not casually recommend using student records, confidential clinic or client material, privileged information, unpublished research data, or personal data. Route these to authorized institutional review and prefer synthetic stand-ins.

## Do not

- Do not authorize restricted-data use.
- Do not invent Stanford approval status or infer it from silence.
- Do not treat an enterprise label as proof of adequate data handling; the current terms must be verified.
