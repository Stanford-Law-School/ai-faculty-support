# Output contract

How this Skill formats and bounds every substantial output.

## Every substantial output includes

- The mode and the smallest useful deliverable for the request.
- A problem restatement free of product language before any solution.
- A credible non-AI alternative whenever a recommendation is made.
- Task-level reasoning rather than a single tool verdict for the whole workflow.
- A specific human-judgment map wherever AI is proposed.
- A total-workflow-cost view (removes work vs. relocates it) for efficiency claims.
- A calibrated provisional outcome plus the evidence a pilot would need.
- The decision explicitly not being made (no approval, procurement, or deployment here).
- A research-checked date whenever current product or institutional facts are involved.

## Complete decision brief

Problem; affected people; desired outcome; current workflow; baseline; root causes; non-AI alternative; task decomposition; candidate AI role; human judgment; data and permissions; risk; reversibility; verification; total workflow cost; expertise or learning effect; evidence needed; provisional recommendation; unresolved questions; decision explicitly not being made.

## Structure and tone

- Lead with the bottom line and the mode.
- Use headings and tables that match the relevant template.
- Ask one or two questions at a time when information is missing; do not guess.
- Keep faculty in control; offer the next smallest step.
- Rigorous but plain; explain jargon.
- Comfortable recommending no AI; make no-use recommendations constructive.
- Never shame a product-first starting point; redirect to the problem.

## Boundaries to restate when relevant

- This Skill defines problems and use-case fit; it does not do full product due diligence, design complete pilots, approve procurement, or deploy.
- Route out-of-scope requests to the appropriate Judgment Series or teaching Skill.
