# PAUSE framework

PAUSE is the SLS Faculty AI Lab's instructional decision framework. It is original faculty guidance, not official Stanford policy. Use it to structure any substantial analysis.

## P — Problem

Define before anything else:

- Intended outcome
- Affected people
- Current workflow
- Friction (where and why it breaks)
- Consequences of the friction
- Baseline (current time, quality, errors, cost, access)
- Constraints (policy, data, resources, deadlines)

## A — AI-fit

Compare candidate approaches on equal footing, not AI against nothing:

- No intervention
- Process improvement
- Documentation
- Training
- Staffing or role clarification
- Search
- Templates
- Spreadsheets
- Databases
- Rules-based automation
- Retrieval
- Generative AI
- Predictive systems
- Constrained agents

## U — Use ethically

Examine confidentiality, privacy, data minimization, permissions, professional responsibility, access, equity, transparency, accountability, and current institutional questions. Flag anything that requires authorized review.

## S — Save time

Count total workflow effort, not just the minutes spent in the tool: preparation, input cleaning, tool interaction, prompting, verification, correction, escalation, coordination, training, maintenance, monitoring, and failure recovery. Ask whether the proposed use removes work or relocates it.

## E — Elevate skills

Ask whether the use strengthens learning, supports judgment, makes reasoning visible, builds verification habits, and expands responsible capacity — or instead encourages dependency, conceals reasoning, removes necessary practice, weakens expertise, and substitutes automation for accountable judgment.

## Using PAUSE

- Move through P and A before U, S, and E; you cannot judge ethics, time, or expertise effects for a problem you have not defined.
- A weak result on any single letter can be decisive (for example, an unacceptable data exposure under U, or lost learning under E), regardless of a strong result elsewhere.
- Record PAUSE findings in plain language, not as a single blended score.
