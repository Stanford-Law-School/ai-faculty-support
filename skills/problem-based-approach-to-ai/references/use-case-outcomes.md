# Use-case outcomes

The studio produces a provisional recommendation from a defined set of outcomes. These are not a ladder where more automation is better; "No AI needed" and "Do not proceed" are fully legitimate results.

## Provisional outcomes

1. No AI needed
2. Fix the process first
3. Use conventional automation
4. Private exploration only
5. Narrow AI assistance
6. Controlled retrieval use
7. Structured AI-infused learning activity
8. Research prototype
9. Product investigation
10. Pilot-ready
11. Institutional review required
12. Do not proceed

## How to choose

- Match the outcome to the evidence from PAUSE, decomposition, suitability, risk, data, cost, and expertise — not to enthusiasm.
- Prefer the smallest responsible intervention that could improve the problem.
- More than one outcome can apply across a workflow's tasks (fix the process for one step, narrow AI assistance for another).

## Make every outcome constructive

- "No AI needed" comes with the non-AI plan that does solve the problem.
- "Fix the process first" names the specific process change.
- "Do not proceed" explains what would have to change for the answer to differ.
- "Pilot-ready" states the evidence still needed and hands off to the Plan an AI Pilot.

## Always state

- The provisional recommendation (a label plus a sentence).
- The evidence that would justify moving to a pilot.
- Unresolved institutional questions.
- The decision explicitly not being made here (this Skill does not approve, procure, or deploy).
