# Total workflow cost

The time a tool appears to save is not the time the workflow saves. Count the whole chain, and ask whether AI removes work or merely relocates it.

## Count every stage

- Preparation
- Input cleaning
- Tool interaction
- Prompting
- Verification
- Correction
- Escalation
- Coordination
- Training (to use the tool well)
- Maintenance
- Monitoring
- Failure recovery

## The relocation test

Ask directly: does this use eliminate work, or move it somewhere else?

- Moving drafting from faculty to AI but adding equal verification time is relocation, not savings.
- Moving effort from a trained expert to an overloaded reviewer can make things worse even if total minutes fall.
- Savings that require constant monitoring and correction may not be savings at all.

## Estimate honestly

- Use ranges and label them as estimates.
- Do not invent time, staffing, cost, or productivity figures.
- Where a baseline exists, compare against it; where it does not, say so.

## Output

Summarize total workflow cost as a short before/after picture with the stages that changed, who bears the new effort, and whether the net effect is a real reduction, a wash, or a relocation. Feed this into the suitability finding and the recommendation.
