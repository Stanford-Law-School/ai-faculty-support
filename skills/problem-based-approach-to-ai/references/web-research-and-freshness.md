# Web research and freshness

When a determination depends on a current product, model, service, price, policy, or institutional fact, research it live rather than relying on memory, and date the finding.

## When live research is required

- A named current product, model, feature, or tier
- Vendor data-handling, retention, or terms
- Productivity or benchmark claims
- Current Stanford services, tiers, or guidance
- Recent studies or reports
- Statements of current substantive law

## Source priority

1. Official Stanford sources
2. Primary legal or regulatory authority
3. Official product documentation
4. Primary research
5. Government or standards sources
6. Independent evaluations
7. Credible reporting

## Method

- Distinguish what a source states from inference.
- Note where sources conflict or are silent.
- Record the research-checked date.

## Freshness note

Any output relying on current facts includes a short note: what was researched, the date, and the confidence and limits.

## Degrade honestly

If research is unavailable, say so, mark the relevant facts as unverified, and do not fabricate current product or institutional details. Recommend confirming before acting.

## Boundaries

- Do not perform full product due diligence (route to the Understand an AI Tool) or claim-adjudication (route to the Check an AI Claim); gather only what the problem determination needs.
- Do not infer approval or prohibition from an absent listing.
