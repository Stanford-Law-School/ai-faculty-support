# Authority Audit Categories

- Source not located
- Wrong source or version
- Incorrect court, jurisdiction, date, or posture
- Quotation or pinpoint problem
- Citation supports only part of the proposition
- Citation concerns the topic but does not support the claim
- Dicta presented as holding
- Party argument presented as court statement
- Historical authority presented as current
- Adverse or limiting treatment
- Binding-status error
- Missing contrary authority
- Overbroad parenthetical or synthesis
- Citation-format issue only

Prioritize substantive support and currentness over cosmetic citation form.
