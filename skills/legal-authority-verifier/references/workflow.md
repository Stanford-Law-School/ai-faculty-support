# Workflow

1. Read the proposition and citation in context.
2. Identify the exact source and retrieve the authoritative text.
3. Confirm identity, court or issuing body, date, jurisdiction, version, and posture.
4. Check the quoted or paraphrased language and pinpoint.
5. Decide whether the source supports the precise proposition, only part of it, or a different proposition.
6. Check binding status and source hierarchy.
7. Check current treatment with an appropriate citator when material.
8. Seek contrary or limiting authority.
9. Classify the issue and propose the smallest correction.
10. Preserve an audit trail and distinguish verified from unverified items.
