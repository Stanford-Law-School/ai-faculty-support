# Routing Workflow

1. Ask what the faculty member is trying to produce or decide.
2. Identify the object of work: question, case, body of doctrine, legal text, history, foreign law, record, agreement, ethics issue, argument, or citations.
3. Identify whether the need is research, teaching, or research-assistant supervision.
4. Check for client, clinic, active-matter, confidential, or live-decision exclusions.
5. Recommend one primary Skill.
6. Add one supporting Skill only when there is a clear dependency.
7. Explain why in two or three sentences.
8. Provide a ready-to-use opening prompt.
9. Do not perform the full routed analysis unless the user asks to continue in that Skill.
