# Route Decision Rules

- The problem is not yet a precise legal question: Legal Question Framer.
- One opinion or a small set of opinions needs close analysis: Case Law Analyzer.
- A body of authorities needs synthesis: Doctrine Mapper.
- The center of gravity is statutory or regulatory text: Statute and Regulation Interpreter.
- The question turns on enactment or rulemaking history: Legislative and Administrative History Navigator.
- The work crosses legal systems or jurisdictions: Comparative Law Research Planner.
- The source set is a docket or litigation record: Litigation Record Analyzer.
- The source set is an agreement or transaction document set: Transaction Structure Analyzer.
- The issue concerns legal ethics for research or teaching: Professional Responsibility Analyzer.
- A developed position needs adversarial testing: Legal Argument Stress Tester.
- Existing citations or authorities need verification: Legal Authority Verifier.
