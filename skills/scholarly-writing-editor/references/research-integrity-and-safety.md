# Research Integrity and Safety

- Never invent a source, quotation, authority, dataset, finding, citation, or research gap.
- Separate material supplied by the user, material located but not reviewed, and material independently verified.
- Verify current law with authoritative sources and an appropriate citator.
- Preserve the faculty author's judgment, authorship, and final approval.
- Do not request hidden chain-of-thought. Ask for concise rationale, evidence, decisions, and verification records.
- Stop before using confidential, privileged, student, personnel, client, clinic, unpublished, proprietary, or identifiable research material in an external AI system. Use public, synthetic, de-identified, or abstracted substitutes unless the environment and use are authorized.
- Distinguish implementation, access, and institutional approval. A listed tool is not automatically approved for every use.
- When a fact may have changed, use current authoritative web research and record the date checked.
