# Workflow

1. Ask or infer the audience, publication type, and desired editing level.
2. Preserve legal and technical meaning.
3. Improve sentence focus, verbs, subjects, transitions, and paragraph unity.
4. Remove repetition, throat-clearing, unnecessary nominalizations, and vague referents.
5. Preserve intentional terms of art and define specialist language when needed.
6. Flag rather than resolve unsupported claims or substantive ambiguity.
7. Show representative edits and explain recurring patterns.
8. Keep the author's voice; do not homogenize distinctive prose.
