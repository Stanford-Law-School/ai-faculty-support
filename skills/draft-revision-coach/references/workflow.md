# Workflow

1. Identify audience, purpose, central claim, and draft stage.
2. Create a reverse outline from the supplied text.
3. Test whether every section has a clear job and advances the argument.
4. Locate buried claims, unsupported transitions, repetition, and evidence appearing too early or late.
5. Check the order of background, method, analysis, counterargument, and implications.
6. Distinguish structural changes from sentence-level edits.
7. Propose a revision sequence with rationale and dependencies.
8. Preserve the author's voice and flag rather than invent missing support.
