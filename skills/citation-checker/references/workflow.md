# Workflow

1. Inventory each citation, quotation, and source-dependent claim.
2. Verify source existence and bibliographic identity.
3. Open the source and compare the cited proposition with the actual text.
4. Check quotation accuracy, omissions, alterations, and pinpoint.
5. Assess source type, authority, jurisdiction, and date.
6. Update legal authority using a citator when required.
7. Flag missing, weak, ambiguous, or overextended support.
8. Separate substantive support issues from citation-style issues.
9. Record verified, not verified, and unable to access.
