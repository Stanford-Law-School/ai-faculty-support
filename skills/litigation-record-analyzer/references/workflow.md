# Workflow

1. Identify the exact matter, court, docket, time period, and faculty purpose.
2. Establish the source and completeness of the docket or supplied record.
3. Create a document ledger with filing date, filer, document type, relief requested, and disposition.
4. Build a procedural chronology.
5. Separate allegations, admissions, disputed evidence, recommendations, findings, holdings, and judgments.
6. Link exhibits and transcripts to the filing or hearing that gives them context.
7. Map issues across motions and rulings.
8. Identify sealed, redacted, missing, superseded, or inaccessible materials.
9. Trace appeals, remands, amendments, and later proceedings.
10. Produce a record map with source citations and completeness limits.
