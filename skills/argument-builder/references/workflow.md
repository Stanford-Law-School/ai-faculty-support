# Workflow

1. Restate the proposed claim precisely and identify its type.
2. List the evidence that actually supports it and the evidence still needed.
3. State the warrant connecting evidence to claim.
4. Identify assumptions, scope conditions, uncertainty, and alternative explanations.
5. Build the strongest good-faith counterargument.
6. Revise the claim to the strongest defensible form.
7. Separate descriptive, causal, interpretive, doctrinal, and normative moves.
8. Identify implications without extending beyond the evidence.
9. Produce an argument map and research needs.
