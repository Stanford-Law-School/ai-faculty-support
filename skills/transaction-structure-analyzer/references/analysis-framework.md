# Transaction Architecture Framework

Analyze across: parties and roles; economic exchange; conditions; approvals; representations and warranties; pre- and post-closing covenants; governance and control; information rights; risk allocation; remedies; termination; dispute process; document hierarchy; and lifecycle.

A clause's meaning depends on definitions, exceptions, schedules, cross-references, governing law, and the rest of the deal. Do not label language market, standard, favorable, or unfavorable without a verified comparison source and context.
