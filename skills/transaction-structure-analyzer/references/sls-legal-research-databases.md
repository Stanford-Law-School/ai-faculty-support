# SLS Legal Research Database Guide

This guide is based on the database information supplied by the Robert Crown Law Library and the accompanying URL workbook. The bundled `sls-database-directory.csv` contains 115 database and collection records with SLS access links, target URLs, product pages, and mapping notes.

## Selection principles

- Start with the legal question, jurisdiction, date, procedural posture, and source type.
- Use controlling primary authority and an appropriate citator when current validity matters.
- Use more than one database when indexing, historical depth, source coverage, or disciplinary reach differs.
- Prefer SLS access links for actual research and public product pages only for product information.
- Treat database AI summaries as leads, not authority.
- Keep a dated research log showing databases, queries, filters, useful sources, dead ends, and updating steps.

## Choose by research task

| Research task | Strong starting points | Important cautions |
|---|---|---|
| Broad U.S. case, statute, regulation, and secondary-source research | Westlaw Precision, Lexis for Law Schools, Bloomberg Law | Verify with the underlying authority and update material authorities with KeyCite, Shepard's, or an appropriate citator. |
| Federal dockets and filings | Bloomberg Law, Lexis CourtLink, Westlaw docket resources | Distinguish allegations, filings, recommendations, orders, judgments, and appellate history. |
| Historical law journals and scanned legal materials | HeinOnline Law Journal Library, Legal Classics, LLMC Digital, Making of Modern Law collections | Historical coverage may not establish current law. |
| Legislative history | ProQuest Congressional, ProQuest Legislative Insight, HeinOnline federal legislative-history collections | Identify the source type and avoid claiming that one item represents the legislature as a whole. |
| Regulatory and administrative history | ProQuest Regulatory Insight, Federal Register and agency sources, HeinOnline CFR collections | Distinguish proposed rules, final rules, guidance, adjudications, and current codified text. |
| Foreign, comparative, and international law | Foreign Law Guide, Index to Foreign Legal Periodicals, Kluwer Law Online, Oxford international-law resources, Jus Mundi, Investor-State LawGuide, TradeLawGuide | Verify official texts, translations, legal status, jurisdiction-specific terminology, and institutional context. |
| Legal scholarship discovery | HeinOnline, Index to Legal Periodicals and Books, LegalTrac, SSRN and Legal Scholarship Network | Working papers and abstracts are not substitutes for reviewing the full source. |
| Supreme Court records and briefs | Supreme Court Insight, Making of Modern Law Supreme Court Records and Briefs, HeinOnline U.S. Supreme Court Library | Verify document identity, date, docket, and relationship to the opinion. |
| Empirical or institutional research | PolicyMap, Gallup Analytics, TRACfed, BankruptcyData, LoPucki Bankruptcy Research Database, litigation analytics tools | Confirm provenance, unit of analysis, coverage, missingness, permissible use, and limits of inference. |
| California practice context | OnLAW / CEB | Practice guidance is secondary material; verify controlling authority and currentness. |
| News and current awareness | Law360, Law.com, Bloomberg Law news, E&E publications, Stanford Law NB | News may identify developments but is not legal authority. |
| Specialized arbitration and investment law | Kluwer Arbitration, Jus Mundi, Investment Arbitration Reporter, Investor-State LawGuide | Check source status, tribunal, language, official text, and subsequent treatment. |

## Access and currentness

Access, licensing, coverage, interfaces, product names, AI features, and authentication methods may change. Before making a definitive statement:

1. Open the SLS access link.
2. Confirm the product and account tier.
3. Check coverage and date information.
4. Verify current law through authoritative sources and a citator when needed.
5. Contact `library@law.stanford.edu` when access or database choice is uncertain.

## Using the full directory

Consult `sls-database-directory.csv` when a specialist resource may fit the project. Search by database name, source type, jurisdiction, subject, vendor, or URL. Preserve the distinction among the SLS access link, unproxied target, public product page, and mapping note.
