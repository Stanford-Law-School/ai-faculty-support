# Workflow

1. Identify whether the request is for scholarship, teaching, or research-assistant supervision.
2. Restate the topic without assuming the answer.
3. Identify the actor, legal relationship, jurisdiction, tribunal, date, and procedural posture.
4. Separate known facts, disputed facts, missing facts, assumptions, and legal characterizations.
5. Identify the legal decision or uncertainty the question must resolve.
6. Draft one primary question and only the subquestions necessary to answer it.
7. Identify controlling source types, useful secondary sources, and currentness requirements.
8. State what the question does not ask.
9. Identify facts or authorities that could change the framing.
10. End with the next research or teaching action.
