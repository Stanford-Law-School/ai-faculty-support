# Question-Framing Framework

## Question layers

- **Context:** What legal setting and audience matter?
- **Actor and authority:** Who acts, under what source of authority, and against whom?
- **Trigger:** Which event, text, decision, or factual condition activates the issue?
- **Legal uncertainty:** What rule, interpretation, classification, procedure, or remedy is unresolved?
- **Boundary:** Which jurisdiction, time, posture, and source set define the inquiry?
- **Decision:** What must the researcher or student be able to conclude?

## Quality checks

A strong legal question is open to more than one answer, does not hide a conclusion in its wording, identifies the governing legal setting, can be answered with attainable authority, and separates law from disputed facts and policy preferences.
