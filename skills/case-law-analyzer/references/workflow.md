# Workflow

1. Confirm the exact opinion, court, date, reporter or docket identifier, and source status.
2. Identify the procedural history and the decision under review.
3. Separate legally material facts from background details and party characterizations.
4. State each issue at the level actually decided.
5. Identify the disposition and each holding necessary to it.
6. Separate reasoning, dicta, assumptions, and unresolved questions.
7. Record the standard of review, burdens, remedy, and institutional posture.
8. Treat majority, concurrence, and dissent separately.
9. Check later treatment when current doctrinal status matters.
10. Produce a case map and a verification record.
