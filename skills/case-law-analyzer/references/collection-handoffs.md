# SLS Legal Skills Route Map

Use one primary Skill whenever practical.

| Need | Primary Skill |
|---|---|
| Define the legal question, jurisdiction, posture, facts, and authority needs | Legal Question Framer |
| Analyze one or more judicial opinions | Case Law Analyzer |
| Synthesize a body of cases into doctrine, tests, exceptions, and splits | Doctrine Mapper |
| Interpret statutory or regulatory text and its surrounding authority | Statute and Regulation Interpreter |
| Plan legislative or administrative history research | Legislative and Administrative History Navigator |
| Plan foreign, comparative, or international-law research | Comparative Law Research Planner |
| Organize and analyze a docket or litigation record for research or teaching | Litigation Record Analyzer |
| Explain agreement architecture and risk allocation for research or teaching | Transaction Structure Analyzer |
| Examine professional-responsibility questions for scholarship or teaching | Professional Responsibility Analyzer |
| Test a legal argument against counterauthority and hidden assumptions | Legal Argument Stress Tester |
| Audit authority, quotations, pinpoints, treatment, and proposition support | Legal Authority Verifier |
| Identify which Legal Skill to begin with | Legal Skills Guide |

Do not perform a complete sibling workflow after routing. Give a brief bridge and a ready-to-use starting prompt.
