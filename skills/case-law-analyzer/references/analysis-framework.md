# Opinion Anatomy

Analyze in this order: identity; court authority; procedural posture; record before the court; material facts; parties' arguments; questions presented; governing sources; standard of review; holdings; reasoning steps; disposition; remedy; dicta; separate opinions; unresolved issues; later treatment.

Do not infer a broad rule from a narrow procedural disposition. Do not convert factual description into a judicial finding unless the opinion does so. Do not use a syllabus or headnote as a substitute for the court's text.
