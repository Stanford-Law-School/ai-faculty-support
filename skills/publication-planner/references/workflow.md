# Workflow

1. Identify the work's audience, contribution, format, length, method, and readiness.
2. Research current venue requirements from official sources.
3. Compare fit, readership, format, timing, access, review model, and practical constraints.
4. Record date checked and unresolved questions.
5. Tailor title, abstract, keywords, and cover materials without overstating claims.
6. Build a submission checklist and workback schedule.
7. Track submissions, decisions, revisions, and withdrawal obligations.
8. Preserve faculty control over venue choice and submission.
