## How to use this list

- Begin with **AI Essentials Training** and **Responsible AI at Stanford** when a task involves institutional AI use.
- Use **Lexis, Westlaw, and Bloomberg** for authoritative legal research and verification; do not rely on generated text alone.
- Treat **Harvey, Legora, LexText, and CICERO** as task-specific assistants whose outputs still require source and professional review.
- Use Stanford enterprise services only within their current documented terms and data-handling boundaries.
- Use the **Request Access Form** for library-provided legal AI tools and `library@law.stanford.edu` for questions.
