# Workflow

1. Define the doctrine, jurisdiction, time frame, court hierarchy, and research purpose.
2. Establish the source set and source-status ledger.
3. Extract propositions at the level actually supported by each authority.
4. Group authorities by rule, element, test, exception, burden, remedy, or procedural gateway.
5. Map agreement, tension, distinction, narrowing, expansion, and overruling.
6. Separate vertical conflict from horizontal disagreement.
7. Identify factual patterns that drive different outcomes.
8. Identify historical stages and institutional rationales.
9. Locate missing source categories and counterauthority.
10. State what the map establishes and what remains uncertain.
