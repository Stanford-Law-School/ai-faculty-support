# Workflow

1. Identify the exact provision, jurisdiction, version, effective date, and research purpose.
2. Retrieve the current official text and relevant prior versions.
3. Read definitions, neighboring provisions, cross-references, exceptions, and enforcement sections.
4. Parse operative verbs, actors, conditions, objects, modifiers, and temporal language.
5. Identify delegated authority and implementing regulations or rules.
6. Locate controlling judicial interpretations and relevant administrative materials.
7. Consider canons only as part of the full interpretive record.
8. Identify amendments, transition rules, savings clauses, and preemption questions.
9. Map plausible interpretations and consequences.
10. State which interpretation is supported, contested, or unresolved.
