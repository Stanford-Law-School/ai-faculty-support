## Five-Minute Teaching Moment (short form)

For a brief in-class moment, compress to: objective; one supplied AI output or demonstration; one thing students must find or verify; one debrief question; what it does not prove.

## Rules

- The plan must name the foundational non-AI work and the no-tool alternative explicitly.
- Timing must include the debrief.
- Do not include grading penalties or misconduct rules; route policy to the Faculty AI Policy Builder.
