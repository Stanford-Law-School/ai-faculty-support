# Web research and freshness

When an activity depends on a current product, service, or institutional fact, research it live and date the finding. Do not rely on memory for product or Stanford facts.

## Require live research for current

- Stanford teaching guidance
- Stanford AI services
- Stanford data classification
- Stanford privacy or security guidance
- SLS course guidance
- Accessibility guidance
- Product availability
- Product terms
- Legal or professional standards

## Source priority

Use official Stanford or SLS sources first, then primary legal or regulatory authority, official product documentation, primary research, government or standards sources, independent evaluations, and credible reporting.

## Freshness note

Any activity that relies on current facts includes a short note: what was researched, the date, and the confidence and limits.

## Degrade honestly

If research is unavailable, say so, mark the relevant facts as unverified, keep the product-independent core of the activity, and do not fabricate current product or institutional details.

## Boundary

Do not perform complete product due diligence (route to the Understand an AI Tool) or adjudicate claims (route to the Check an AI Claim); gather only what the teaching activity needs, and keep the durable concept product-independent.
