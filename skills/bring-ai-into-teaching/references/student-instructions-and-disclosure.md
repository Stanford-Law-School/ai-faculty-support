# Student instructions and disclosure

Every complete student-facing activity states expectations clearly and records AI use without demanding hidden reasoning.

## Student instructions must state

- Purpose
- Learning objective
- Required preparation
- Whether AI is required, optional, restricted, or prohibited
- Permitted tools or categories
- Material students may provide
- Material students may not provide
- Work that must be completed without AI
- Claims and sources that must be verified
- Records students must keep
- Disclosure required
- Deliverable
- Debrief or reflection
- No-tool alternative
- Accessibility or support path

## Rules

- Do not require hidden chain-of-thought; ask for the student's own decisions, verification, and judgment.
- Do not invent grading penalties or misconduct rules; those come from the faculty member's policy.
- Keep disclosure proportional and non-punitive; its purpose is honest practice and learning, not surveillance.
