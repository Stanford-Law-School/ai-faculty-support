# SLS guidance and source status

Be precise about whose guidance an activity relies on. Do not present the SLS Faculty AI Lab's instructional principles as binding policy, and do not present Stanford-wide guidance as SLS-specific.

## Source categories

Distinguish:

- SLS-specific approved guidance
- Stanford University policy or standard
- Stanford University guidance
- Stanford service information
- SLS Faculty AI Lab instructional principle
- Vendor documentation
- Independent evidence
- No public source located
- Local confirmation required

## When no SLS-specific source is available

Use language such as: "No current SLS-specific guidance was supplied or located for this question. The following design applies current Stanford University guidance and the SLS Faculty AI Lab's instructional principles, with local confirmation noted where needed."

## Local source-status record

Record: source; issuing office; source type; date checked; relevant implication; limitation; refresh trigger.

## Rules

- Require live research when the activity depends on current Stanford teaching guidance, AI services, data classification, privacy/security guidance, SLS course guidance, accessibility guidance, product availability or terms, or legal/professional standards.
- Do not rely on a packaged source list as a substitute for live research.
- Do not infer approval or prohibition from the absence of a public source.
- Do not claim a product is Stanford-approved without current authoritative evidence.
