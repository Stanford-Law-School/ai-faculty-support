# Data lifecycle and minimization

Govern data across its whole life, and prefer the least information that can serve the purpose. This Skill identifies data questions; it does not authorize data use.

## Lifecycle stages

1. Collection
2. Input
3. Transmission
4. Storage
5. Retrieval
6. Model processing
7. Tool or connector use
8. Logging
9. Human access
10. Product improvement
11. Model training
12. Subprocessors
13. Export
14. Retention
15. Deletion
16. Archiving

## Minimization questions

- Is the information necessary at all?
- Can less information be used?
- Can public or synthetic material be used instead?
- Can information be de-identified — and is de-identification meaningful in this context?
- Does the product tier change data handling?
- Are logs creating a second sensitive dataset?
- Can deletion be verified?
- Is a secondary purpose quietly developing?

## Do not assume

- "No training" means no retention.
- "Enterprise" means approved.
- "Open-weight" means private.
- Encryption resolves appropriateness.
- Contract terms guarantee technical behavior.

## Boundary

Flag data-classification, retention, and privacy questions for authorized institutional review; do not authorize use or certify privacy. Prefer public or synthetic material for simulation and early stages.
