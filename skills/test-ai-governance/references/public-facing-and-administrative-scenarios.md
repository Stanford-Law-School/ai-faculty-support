# Public-facing and administrative scenarios

Fictional composite patterns for governance where a system reaches the public or acts on institutional records, email, calendars, or drives. Public reach and write access raise the stakes and the need for confirmation, logging, and stop authority. Label every scenario fictional and non-identifying.

## Scenario patterns

### Public information / FAQ assistant
Governance focus: who owns wrong public answers; grounding and fabrication; correction and contestability; transparency that it is AI; monitoring of error categories.

### Shared-drive retrieval agent
Governance focus: least privilege (not whole-drive access); read-only first; scope to minimum records; prompt-injection exposure; logging and revocation.

### Email-drafting or sending agent
Governance focus: distinguish draft from send; human confirmation before external send; misdirected-communication risk; reversibility; audit logs; emergency stop.

### Automated prioritization workflow
Governance focus: ranking is consequential; equity and uneven outcomes; human ownership of decisions; contestability; likely institutional review.

### Agent modifying internal records
Governance focus: write access boundaries; confirmation before modification; reversibility; audit trail; who corrects erroneous changes.

## Cross-cutting requirements

- Confirmation precedes consequential external or irreversible action.
- Public or record-changing use needs a named owner for errors and a real stop mechanism.
- Broad drive/mailbox access for convenience is a governance gap.
