# Incident-response template

Plan how problems are classified, who acts, and how the use resumes — before the use begins.

## Incident classification and actions

| Incident | Immediate action | Owner role | Escalation | Resume condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Include at least one row each for a routine correction, a material incident, and a critical incident.

## Tiers (reminder)

- **Routine correction** — reversible, limited; handled in normal review.
- **Material incident** — consequential error, misleading omission, access problem, repeated failure, or correction failure; requires escalation.
- **Critical incident** — restricted-data exposure; unauthorized external action; fabricated authority used consequentially; incorrect high-stakes determination; permission escalation; untraceable action; significant accessibility exclusion; repeated critical failure after mitigation.

## Stop and resume

- Name who may pause a user, pause a pilot, disable a connector, revoke permissions, and stop external action.
- State who decides resumption and what evidence is required first.
- Treat the absence of a practical stop mechanism as a blocking gap for consequential uses.

## Evidence and redress

- Preserve evidence before remediation.
- Correct downstream effects, not only the original output.
- Log the incident and the response; assign who reviews recurring patterns.

## Boundary

Do not invent institutional incident-reporting obligations; mark them "institutional confirmation required" where unknown, and design a reasonable interim path.
