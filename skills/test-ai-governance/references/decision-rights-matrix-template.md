# Decision-rights matrix template

The decision-rights matrix makes explicit who recommends, reviews, approves, operates, may stop, and is affected for each decision in the use. It does not infer real institutional authority.

## Decision-Rights Matrix

| Decision | Recommends | Reviews | Approves | Operates | May stop | Affected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Decisions to include

Define purpose; select data; select sources; select product; configure model; grant access; enable connector; permit external action; approve exploration; approve pilot; review output; resolve incident; communicate with affected people; change scope; continue use; suspend use; retire the system; delete data.

## Rules

- Do not infer real authority. Mark each cell as Known role, Proposed role, or Institutional confirmation required.
- A decision with no one who "may stop" it is a governance gap.
- "Affected" should name real categories of people (students, clients, participants, public), not be left blank.
- Approval of a pilot is not approval of deployment; keep those as separate decisions.

## Use

Build the matrix during the roles/decision step of a full governance analysis; unresolved cells become items in the governance-gap register and questions for institutional discussion.
