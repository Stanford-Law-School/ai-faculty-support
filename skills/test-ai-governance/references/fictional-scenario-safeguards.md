# Fictional scenario safeguards

Simulations use fictional composite scenarios only. Keep them clearly labeled and never attributed to real people or internal proposals.

## Required label

Every scenario includes:

"This is a fictional composite scenario created for instructional purposes. It is not based on or attributed to an identified Stanford individual, office, committee, student, client, or proposal."

## Do not attribute scenarios to

The Dean; named SLS leaders; named faculty; named staff; named students; real clients; current committees; current internal proposals. Do not closely parody a recognizable internal suggestion.

## Use neutral placeholders

"a seminar instructor," "a clinic supervisor," "a research assistant," "Vendor A," "the authorized institutional decision role — confirmation required."

## When the user provides a real internal scenario

- Use only the facts supplied.
- Do not infer motives or embellish.
- Do not search confidential internal details publicly.
- Use neutral role descriptions.
- Keep unresolved authority as a placeholder rather than assigning it to a real person or office.

## Purpose limit

Scenarios teach governance reasoning; they are not evidence about a real product, office, decision, or person, and they do not attribute views to real individuals.
