# Clinic and sensitive contexts

Fictional composite patterns for the highest-sensitivity settings — clinics, privileged material, real clients or participants, and consequential individual outcomes. Default to strong caution and, often, "do not proceed in current form." Label every scenario fictional and non-identifying.

## What makes these contexts special

- Privilege and confidentiality obligations.
- Real people who did not choose to be part of an AI experiment.
- Consequential, sometimes irreversible, outcomes.
- Professional-responsibility duties that cannot be delegated to a model.

## Governance emphasis

- Least privilege and least data, with a strong presumption toward synthetic or public stand-ins for any exploration.
- No consequential external action without accountable human ownership and confirmation.
- Explicit stop authority and incident response before any use.
- Institutional and ethical review is typically required; mark it as required rather than assuming it.
- Contestability and correction for affected individuals.

## Common finding

For genuinely sensitive or privileged workflows, the well-supported governance finding is frequently "institutional review required" or "the use should not proceed in its current form." Present that as a responsible result, not a failure.

## Boundaries

Do not authorize use of privileged or confidential material, certify compliance, or perform legal or privacy review. Route confidential-material and professional-responsibility questions to authorized review.
