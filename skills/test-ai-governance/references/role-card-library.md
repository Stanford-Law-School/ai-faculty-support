# Role-card library

Reusable fictional roles for simulations. For each, identify responsibilities, information required, decisions owned, decisions not owned, authority to reject, authority to escalate, authority to stop (when applicable), and potential conflicts or limitations. Use placeholders for any institutional authority that is not currently verified.

## Roles

- **Faculty project owner** — accountable for purpose and result; owns whether to propose the use; does not own institutional approval; may stop their own project.
- **Faculty user** — operates the system for their work; owns their outputs; does not own others' data or institutional authorization.
- **Student** — affected person; owns their own work and the right to contest; owns no system decision; needs notice, alternative, and a correction path.
- **Research assistant** — operates workflows under faculty direction; owns careful execution; does not own scholarly accountability.
- **Data steward** — responsible for the information used; owns data-handling questions; can flag and escalate; may withhold data.
- **Technical operator** — configures and runs the system; owns implementation; does not own appropriate-use or purpose decisions.
- **Privacy reviewer** — assesses privacy (authorized process); can require changes or halt on privacy grounds.
- **Security reviewer** — assesses security (authorized process); can require changes or halt on security grounds.
- **Accessibility reviewer** — assesses accessibility; can flag exclusion as a blocking issue.
- **Affected community member** — a member of the public or a group affected by outputs; owns the right to understand and contest; owns no system decision.
- **Vendor representative** — provides the product and support; owns product claims to be verified; owns no institutional decision and no evaluation judgment.
- **Authorized institutional decision role** — may approve pilots, deployment, or data use (outside this Skill); mark as "confirmation required" unless verified.

## Rule

Do not assign real Stanford employees, offices, or titles authority without current verified information. Mark each role Known, Proposed, or Institutional confirmation required.
