# Research and scholarship scenarios

Fictional composite patterns for governance in faculty research. Protect source integrity, unpublished and confidential data, research-participant interests, and scholarly accountability. Label every scenario fictional and non-identifying.

## Scenario patterns

### Research-assistant summary workflow
Governance focus: who owns the scholarly result; source integrity and proposition support; whether variability undermines reproducibility; realistic human verification; the RA's and faculty member's accountability.

### Sensitive or unpublished research material
Governance focus: data minimization; do not upload unpublished interviews or confidential datasets without authorized handling; retention and training questions; prefer public or synthetic corpora.

### Public faculty-scholarship archive
Governance focus: what may be read, retrieved, and published; correction path for errors; attribution integrity; who owns public-facing mistakes.

### Legal or clinic-related information
Governance focus: privilege and confidentiality; least privilege; likely institutional and ethical review; a legitimate outcome is not to proceed.

## Cross-cutting requirements

- The faculty member remains the scholar of record; AI informs, it does not own the judgment.
- Untraceable quotations and fabricated authority are critical concerns.
- Reproducibility requires recording configuration and dates.
- Confidential material requires authorized handling; default to synthetic stand-ins.
