# Web research and freshness

When a governance answer depends on current Stanford guidance or current product behavior, research it live from authoritative sources and record the date. Do not rely on memory for institutional or product facts.

## Require live research for current

- Stanford privacy standards
- Stanford information-security guidance
- Stanford data classification
- Stanford AI services
- Stanford agentic-AI guidance
- Stanford teaching guidance
- Stanford research guidance
- Stanford accessibility guidance
- Stanford approval or review processes
- Current product capabilities or terms

## Source priority and categories

Use official Stanford sources first. Distinguish clearly:

- SLS-specific approved guidance
- Stanford University policy or standard
- Stanford University guidance
- Stanford service information
- SLS Faculty AI Lab instructional principle
- Vendor claim
- Independent evidence
- No public source located
- Institutional confirmation required

## Uncertain-status wording

"I did not locate a current public Stanford source establishing that exact authority or approval. That does not establish that the use is approved or prohibited. Confirmation is needed for the specific data, account, purpose, permissions, and workflow."

## Source map fields

Record: source title; issuing office; URL; source type; relevant governance principle; date checked; limitation; refresh trigger.

## Do not

- Infer authority from an office name.
- Describe Stanford University guidance as SLS-specific.
- Treat a packaged source map as a substitute for current research.
- Treat absence of a public listing as approval or prohibition.
