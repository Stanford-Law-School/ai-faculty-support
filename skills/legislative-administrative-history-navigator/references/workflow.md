# Workflow

1. Identify the enactment, amendment, regulation, jurisdiction, date range, and precise interpretive question.
2. Build a chronology of official actions and versions.
3. Identify source types and the institutional actor responsible for each.
4. Retrieve official materials before relying on summaries.
5. Compare text changes across versions.
6. Separate explanations, advocacy, testimony, analysis, and formal findings.
7. For rulemaking, distinguish proposal, comments, agency response, final rule, and later guidance or adjudication.
8. Evaluate relevance and weight for the stated interpretive method.
9. Record missing materials and search limits.
10. Produce a source plan or history map with calibrated conclusions.
