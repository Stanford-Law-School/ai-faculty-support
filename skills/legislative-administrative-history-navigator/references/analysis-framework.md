# History Source Framework

## Legislative materials

Bill text and versions; committee reports; hearings; sponsor statements; floor debate; conference materials; enrolled bill; signing or veto materials; implementation records.

## Administrative materials

Statutory delegation; advance notice; proposed rule; preamble; comments; studies; regulatory analysis; agency responses; final rule; technical corrections; guidance; adjudications; enforcement materials.

For each item record authorship, date, official status, relationship to the final text, and what it can and cannot establish.
