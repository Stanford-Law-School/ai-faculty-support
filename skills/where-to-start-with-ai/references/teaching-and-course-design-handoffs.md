# Teaching and Course Design handoffs

When the faculty member needs a **complete teaching deliverable**, route from Bring AI Into Your Teaching context to the appropriate Teaching and Course Design Skill.

## Handoff table

| Faculty need | Destination Skill |
|---|---|
| Entire course or syllabus | Course and Syllabus Designer |
| One class session | Class Session Planner |
| Assignment, exam, or rubric | Assessment and Rubric Builder |
| Course or assignment AI rules | Faculty AI Policy Builder |
| Questions or hypotheticals | Socratic Question and Hypothetical Designer |
| Accessibility audit | Teaching Materials Accessibility Reviewer |
| Reading and course materials | Reading List and Course Materials Curator |

## Routing logic

| Prompt pattern | Primary route | Handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Should students use AI for this assignment? | Bring AI Into Your Teaching | Policy Builder, Assessment Builder as needed |
| Redesign my entire course around AI | Course and Syllabus Designer | Bring AI Into Your Teaching may precede for judgment framing |
| I need rules for student AI use | Faculty AI Policy Builder | No forced orientation diagnostic |
| Is this AI activity accessible? | Bring AI Into Your Teaching for activity design; Accessibility Reviewer for full audit | Explain distinction |
| Readings about AI and legal practice | Reading List and Course Materials Curator | Not Understand an AI Tool unless products are the subject |

## Handoff format

Provide:

- Destination name
- Why it fits
- Ready-to-use opening prompt
- Expected artifact
- Likely follow-up

Do not duplicate the destination workflow.

## Collection relationship

The SLS AI Judgment Series builds judgment about AI systems and evidence. The Teaching and Course Design collection produces course artifacts. This guide explains the relationship without merging workflows.
