# Stress-Test Lenses

- Authority and hierarchy
- Proposition support
- Counterauthority and exceptions
- Factual predicates and alternative characterizations
- Procedure and standard of review
- Remedy and redressability
- Institutional role and competence
- Administrability and limiting principles
- Reliance and transition effects
- Equality, access, and distributional consequences
- Historical and comparative counterexamples
- Strongest fair opposing position

Do not use a straw opponent. State the strongest version an informed critic could defend.
