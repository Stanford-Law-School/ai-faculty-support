# Workflow

1. State the argument as a clear claim with its requested legal consequence.
2. Identify the authority and facts each step requires.
3. Separate doctrinal, factual, normative, institutional, and remedial premises.
4. Build the strongest fair opposing argument.
5. Seek counterauthority, exceptions, adverse treatment, and alternative characterizations.
6. Test the standard of review and procedural posture.
7. Test limiting principles, administrability, reliance, and unintended consequences.
8. Identify facts that would reverse or narrow the conclusion.
9. Revise the claim, scope, or support rather than merely adding rhetoric.
10. Produce a stress-test record and prioritized revision plan.
