# Workflow

Ask one or two questions: What are you working on? What is blocking progress now? What artifact do you have?

Route primarily to one helper:
- Research Project Planner: question, scope, method, feasibility, roadmap.
- Literature Review Helper: source discovery, matrix, synthesis, gap verification.
- Legal Research Planner: authoritative legal source strategy and updating.
- Argument Builder: claims, evidence, warrants, counterarguments.
- Draft Revision Coach: structure and argument flow.
- Citation Checker: existence, support, quotations, authority, currency.
- Scholarly Writing Editor: sentence and paragraph clarity.
- Publication Planner: venue and submission planning.
- Public Writing Adapter: adaptation for new audiences.

Offer a second route only when it is the natural next dependency.
