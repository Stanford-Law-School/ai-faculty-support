# Workflow

1. Define the legal question, jurisdiction, tribunal, date, and procedural posture.
2. Identify controlling primary sources and useful secondary sources.
3. Choose SLS databases based on source type and coverage.
4. Develop terms of art, broader/narrower concepts, citations, parties, and docket identifiers.
5. Search primary authority, then expand through headnotes, citing references, annotations, and secondary sources.
6. Seek contrary authority, exceptions, and jurisdictional variation.
7. Update every material authority with the appropriate citator.
8. Maintain a dated research log and unresolved-questions list.
9. State what has and has not been verified.
