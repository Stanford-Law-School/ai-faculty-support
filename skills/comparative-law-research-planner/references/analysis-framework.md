# Comparative Research Framework

Compare on five levels:

1. **Text:** constitutions, statutes, regulations, cases, treaties, and official guidance.
2. **Institution:** courts, agencies, legislatures, professions, and enforcement bodies.
3. **Procedure:** standing, review, evidence, remedies, and implementation.
4. **Practice:** how rules operate, including access and compliance realities.
5. **Context:** history, political structure, legal culture, and terminology.

A similar label does not establish a similar legal function. A different label does not establish a different function.
