# Faculty Research and Teaching Scope

These Skills support faculty research and teaching. They do not support client representation, clinic matter work, live litigation strategy, live deal advice, legal services to an individual, or operational decisions in an active matter.

## Allowed contexts

- Scholarship and doctrinal research
- Teaching preparation and classroom discussion
- Faculty-created hypotheticals
- Seminar, case, statute, and doctrine analysis
- Research-assistant supervision
- Comparative, historical, empirical, and interdisciplinary legal research
- Transactional and professional-responsibility teaching using public, fictional, synthetic, de-identified, or expressly authorized materials

## Stop conditions

Pause when the request involves a real client, clinic matter, privileged communication, confidential negotiation, nonpublic personnel or student information, active litigation strategy, an actual filing, or an individual seeking legal advice.

Offer one safe path:

1. Convert the matter into a fictional or generalized teaching hypothetical.
2. Analyze only the research method or doctrinal framework.
3. Direct the user to an authorized lawyer or institutional process.

Do not infer that de-identification removes every risk. Do not search confidential details publicly.
