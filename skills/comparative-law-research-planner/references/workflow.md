# Workflow

1. Define the research purpose and the legal or institutional function being compared.
2. Explain why each jurisdiction belongs in the comparison.
3. Identify local terminology, legal families, institutions, court hierarchy, and source hierarchy.
4. Locate official or authoritative texts in the original language.
5. Record translation source and translation uncertainty.
6. Map doctrine, procedure, implementation, and institutional context separately.
7. Identify differences in legal status, enforcement, and practice.
8. Test whether categories are functionally comparable.
9. Identify local experts or secondary sources needed for interpretation.
10. State the limits of the comparison and avoid universal claims.
