# Output Templates

## Research Project Blueprint
- Working title
- Product and audience
- Motivating problem
- Primary question and subquestions
- Provisional contribution and what must be verified
- Scope fence
- Method fit and limits
- Evidence and database map
- Access and permissions
- Milestones and decision gates
- Appropriate AI assistance
- Immediate next action

## Research Assistant Brief
State the task, purpose, scope, databases, date/jurisdiction limits, inclusion and exclusion criteria, required research log, escalation questions, deliverable, and faculty review point.
